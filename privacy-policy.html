Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Mëso Programimin collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you use our platform.

1) Information We Collect

  • Account information such as name, username, and email address.
  • Authentication-related data handled through Supabase (login, session, recovery).
  • Learning activity data such as lesson progress and completed modules.

2) How We Use Information

  • To create and secure your account.
  • To provide course progress tracking and personalized learning experience.
  • To communicate important account or security updates.

3) Data Sharing

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We only share data with service providers required to operate the platform (for example, Supabase for authentication/storage).

4) Data Retention

We keep account and learning data as long as your account is active, or as needed to provide the service. You may request account deletion, after which related personal data is removed according to our deletion process.

5) Your Rights

  • You can request password reset and account access updates.
  • You can request account deletion from your profile settings.
  • You can contact us for data-related questions.

6) Contact

For privacy questions, email us at support@mesoprogramim.online.
